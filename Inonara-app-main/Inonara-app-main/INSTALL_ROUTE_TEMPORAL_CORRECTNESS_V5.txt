INONARA — Route temporal correctness v5

This correction stops publishing automatically-derived diaspora routes whose
route-level dates have not been verified. It preserves every candidate in an
editorial inventory, but the public Atlas receives only curated macro-routes
and explicitly reviewed diaspora routes.

Reason: a diaspora community existing in 2025 does not mean the migration
movement that formed it is active in 2025.

No candidate date has been changed or invented.
